Cloakpaste

Hides secrets and personal data before they reach AI chats. Detection runs on this device; nothing is uploaded.

You're set up

Cloakpaste now checks what you paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other AI chats. Emails, keys, card numbers and names are swapped for placeholders like EMAIL_1 before the text lands. When the AI replies with a placeholder, hover it to see the original, or copy the reply to get the originals back.
